🎸 Body Builder

A small guitar body for 3- and 4-string necks, printed as one piece: a rim and a solid back floor, printed floor down with the open side up. A ⅛″ plywood top drops into the rim and glues onto a shelf that runs round the inside. The neck screws into an integral, ribbed neck block. Optional access panel in the back.

Printing and assembly
  • Print the body back face down, open side up, as one part. No supports: the walls and the neck block go straight up, and the underside of the glue shelf is a 45° staircase (0.4 mm steps). With screw wells on, the flat round roof of each well is a short bridge.
  • Material: PETG (or ASA) — it creeps less than PLA under string and screw load. 0.2 mm layers.
  • 7 walls / perimeters (0.4 mm nozzle): the 3 mm rim and the shelf print fully solid. 6 top and bottom layers, so the 3 mm back floor is solid too.
  • Neck block: a ribbed shell (pocket walls, a plate under the pocket and screw bosses; with “Neck block ribs” on, one brace across the body through it), so normal infill (15–25%) is fine — the 7 perimeters make its walls solid. The cells under the pocket are sealed and close at 45°, no supports.
  • Top: laser-cut the ⅛″ ply from the soundboard SVG / DXF / PDF (1:1, millimetres; check the 100 mm square first, then hide the calibration layer). Red = outer cut, blue = inner cuts (sound hole, screw holes), black = engrave. The outer edge is cut a hair big (kerf / 2 + the press fit) so it presses into the rim and sits on the shelf; the notch fits round the neck pocket. Glue it to the shelf and the top of the neck block (wood glue or epoxy), weighted flat — or screw it down if the screw-on top is on.
  • Ladder braces (on by default): tall, thin, with a parabolic rounded top; they print straight up from the floor in 0.5 mm steps that only ever get narrower, so no supports.
  • Screw-on top (if on): leave the glue off; screw down through the top into the tabs under the shelf with coarse-thread wood / plastic screws (#4 × ½″ pan head to start). When a hole wears, re-drill the tab for #6, then #8, and pick that size here so the top's holes match. The tabs' undersides are 45° tapers, no supports.
  • Neck: drill pilot holes in the neck through the body's holes, then screw on from the back (through the back plate, or with washers / ferrules in the wells). Snug, not cranked.
  • Access panel (if on): print the cover plate face down (the STL is the right way up). It drops into the bevelled recess flush with the back and is held by small screws into the ring round the opening.

All sizes in millimetres in the files. One body STL, plus the cover plate when the access panel is on. See README.md in the repo for the design notes.
