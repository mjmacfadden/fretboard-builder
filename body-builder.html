🎸 Body Builder

A small acoustic-style body for 3- and 4-string guitars: a printed rim in two mirror-image halves with tracks for ⅛″ plywood top and back panels. Slide the panels into one half, close the other half over them and bolt the halves together through the solid neck and tail blocks. The neck bolts on with four screws through a back plate.

Printing and assembly
  • Print each half back face down (the flat side that was on the bed is the back). No supports: every overhang is a 45° staircase except the underside of three channel lips (both lips of the top channel and the upper lip of the back channel). Those are flat ledges as wide as the lip overlap (4 mm by default) off the wall with nothing under them, so turn on your slicer's bridge / overhang cooling. Each channel is the sag allowance (0.3 mm by default) taller than panel + fit, so a little droop won't pinch the panel.
  • Neck block and tail block: separate parts. Print each standing on its flat underside (the STL is already the right way up); that face sits on the back panel. The upper parts step in with 45° staircases, so no supports.
  • Neck collar (split neck block): print it flat underside down — the STL is already the right way up. It carries the pocket's side and end walls; the pocket floor is the top of the neck block, so the neck screws clamp only solid block.
  • Material: PETG (or ASA) — it creeps less than PLA under constant string and screw load. 0.2 mm layers.
  • 7 walls / perimeters (0.4 mm nozzle, ~0.45 mm lines): the 3 mm rim wall and the channel lips print fully solid. 6 top and bottom layers; 30–40% gyroid only where the flanges are wider than that.
  • Neck and tail blocks: use a slicer modifier (height-range or box modifier) for 80–100% infill in the blocks — this is the material the neck screws and bolts clamp.
  • Plywood: cut the panels from the SVG/DXF (1:1, millimetres; check the 100 mm bar after printing a paper copy). The top panel has the neck-pocket notch. The back panel is the full outline, with holes for the neck screws and for the heads of the collar bolts; drill them where the SVG/DXF marks them.
  • Assembly: bolt the collar onto the neck block (collar bolts up from the block's underside into the nuts or inserts). Lay the back panel flat and stand the neck block and tail block on it, on their outlines. Lay the top panel on the block tops. Slide one half in sideways so both panel edges enter its channels and its bolt bosses enter the block notches, then the other half from the other side. Put in the neck bolt and the tail bolts (washers both sides). The blocks sit a little loose on the back panel (the channel's fit and sag room) until everything is clamped. Glue the panels into the channels if you like — it isn't needed to hold them.
  • Neck: drill pilot holes in the neck through the body's holes, then screw on through the back plate and the back panel. Snug, not cranked.

All sizes in millimetres in the files. Both half STLs are needed (they are mirror images except on cutaway styles), plus the neck collar when the neck block is split. See README.md in the repo for the design notes.
